About

Mechanical engineer turned AI builder

I'm Toni Esan, based in London. I work across engineering, AI and creative product thinking. I'm not a pure researcher and not a pure developer. I build things that bridge disciplines and solve real problems with taste.

Completed an MSc in Applied AI at Cranfield (Distinction, July 2025) while working as Technical Officer at Leveret AI, a CV startup bringing AI diagnostics to NHS dental clinics. Before that, I was working in industry as a mechanical engineer, honing in on software engineering skills and web automations.

I've worked with autonomous vehicles since 2019, before Waymo made self-driving mainstream. I've grown social accounts to half a million followers, built dental AI products used in real clinics, and now run my own AI consulting practice. I wear a lot of hats, literally.

BSL, conversational Spanish and Yoruba, basic Arabic. Currently training for a half marathon. Running is the thinking time.
