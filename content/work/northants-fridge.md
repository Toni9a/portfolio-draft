> A used fridge and freezer seller in Northampton sold mostly through Facebook. I built a site that opens like a fridge and shows the stock on lit shelves, so people can find them on Google.

## [THE PROBLEM] Stock only on Facebook

Northants Fridge sold through Facebook Marketplace. That worked for people already following, but nobody searching Google for "used fridge freezer Northampton" would find them. The main goal was a site of their own for current stock and free collection, set up for local search.

## [MY PART] Direction, build and search

I built the whole site. I set the visual direction in ChatGPT and used Gemini for the animation.

[[image:nf-hero]]

## [HOW IT WORKS] Open the door, browse the shelves

- **Scroll to open.** The fridge door opens frame by frame as you scroll, and the light comes on inside.
- **Stock on the shelves.** Current appliances sit on lit shelves, each linking to its listing.
- **Facebook import.** Stock is imported from their Facebook Marketplace listings.
- **Collection info.** Free collection and how to get in touch, on every page.

[[image:nf-shelves]]

## [DESIGN DECISION] The fridge is the menu

>> The inside of the fridge is how you get around.

The early brief explored using the fridge's interior as the navigation. A used appliance shop is easy to forget. A fridge that opens when you scroll isn't.

## [SEARCH] Local SEO from day one

The site has a sitemap, page metadata and structured data for a local business, so Google can show their stock and location.

## [WHERE IT IS] Live and in use

The shop is using it now. [Visit the site](https://northants-fridge.vercel.app/).

Built with Next.js on Vercel, Gemini for animation, ChatGPT for visual planning, a Facebook Marketplace import and local SEO.
