> Fitwithfaith's followers wanted one place for scripture, training and food. I designed and built her a native iPhone app, and six people are testing it now.

## [THE PROBLEM] Three apps for one routine

Fitwithfaith's TikTok audience comes for two things: faith and fitness. Following along meant a Bible app for verses, notes for stretching and gym tips, and something else for meal planning. The aim was one app where a follower can read a verse, plan the week and log a session in the same place.

## [MY PART] Design, build and creative direction

I designed it, built it and worked with her on what it should be. I also led the creative direction for the look and the onboarding. I put the beta onto six of her friends' phones and re-sign the builds each week from my laptop so they keep working.

## [HOW IT WORKS] A week, a verse and a log

- **Week planner.** Lay out training, rest and meals for the week ahead.
- **Strength logging.** Log sets and see progress over time with charts.
- **Live Activities.** A session in progress sits on the lock screen.
- **Scripture.** Search verses, save the ones that land and come back to them.
- **Journal.** Write a few lines after reading or training.

## [DESIGN DECISION] The Faith Line onboarding

>> Tap what matters to you, and the onboarding jumps straight to that scene.

Onboarding is one continuous line through a series of scenes. When a new user taps a priority, the line jumps to the scene for it. I made the videos in stages so each one flows into the next. I tried Google Flow for the animation first, then moved to Midjourney for most of the moodboarding and video. My time in Midjourney helped me land on something that looked good and looked original, not like a template.

## [STACK] Native iOS

SwiftUI, SwiftData, Xcode, ActivityKit for Live Activities and Swift Charts for progress. There is also a browser walkthrough for anyone without the beta.

## [WHERE IT IS] Private beta

Six testers are using it on their phones. It isn't on the App Store yet. [Try the browser demo](https://fitfai.vercel.app/demo/).
