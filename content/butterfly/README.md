# Butterfly homepage preview (local only)

`index-butterfly.html` = the live `index.html` + the Codex hydrangea butterfly system
(source: `~/Documents/Codex/2026-08-25/thi/outputs/butterfly-animation-study/ANIMATION-HANDOFF.md`).
`index.html` is never touched. Everything here is in `.git/info/exclude`.

Run:

    cd ~/"personal port"
    python3 content/butterfly/build_butterfly_page.py   # re-run after editing index.html
    python3 -m http.server 8010
    open http://localhost:8010/index-butterfly.html

What it does:
- Hero: the rotated flower card is replaced by a cut-out of the transition hydrangea (`assets/hydrangea-cutout.webp`, black keyed to alpha, no blend modes, so nothing gets clipped). In the hero each butterfly surfaces from under the loose left-edge petals, small, and flies outwards as it fades (`--drift: 1`).
- Hat links (contracting, thinking...) recolour the flock, scroll to the section and settle the butterflies around its project grid.
- Every butterfly fades in and out of the dark on its own cycle (`bf-glimmer`), so they're never all visible at once.
- The flock layer sits behind the page (z-index -1). Cards are slightly see-through with a 3px blur, so a butterfly behind one reads as a soft glow.
- In the work sections only two butterflies are out (a different pair per hat). They drift behind visible project cards, and every 7s one moves to another card. Hovering a card sends the nearer one behind it.
- Natural scrolling picks the hat section nearest mid-screen and recolours the pair. Long sections (engineering) follow whichever grid is in view.
- Bottom-left toggle: Ink (black) / Paper (dark paper texture, `assets/paper-dark.webp`, tileable 512px). Choice is remembered in localStorage.
- Third option, Kraft: cardboard (`assets/kraft.webp`, 1024px, faint corrugation) plus a sparse layer of pencil and chalk sketches (`assets/sketches.webp`, 1600px: butterflies, gears, cubes, dimension lines, wireframes, hydrangea florets). The sketches scroll with the page; faint text that sits directly on the board is lifted. Both generated by `make_kraft.py`.
- Past the work section (AI-built, consulting, about) the flock fades out; it hides while a project modal is open.
- prefers-reduced-motion pauses everything.

Assets: `assets/hydrangea-cutout.webp` (411 KB, alpha cut-out of the 2.2 MB PNG; `hydrangea-transition.webp` is the old unused opaque version), `assets/butterfly-sprite.webp` (108 KB, 768x512), `assets/paper-dark.webp` (generated, tileable).
